# Project Details

## Overview

UNIFY is an AI-powered learning platform that brings educational resources from different platforms into one centralized and organized space. Students often struggle to find and manage study materials such as notes, PDFs, YouTube videos, GitHub repositories, textbooks, and previous-year question papers scattered across the internet.
UNIFY aims to simplify this process by helping students discover, organize, access, and learn from educational resources in one place. With AI-powered assistance, collaborative study features, interactive quizzes, and progress tracking, the platform makes learning more accessible, organized, and engaging.

## Problem Statement

Notes, PDFs, YouTube videos, GitHub repositories, previous-year questions, and textbooks are often scattered across different platforms, making it difficult for students to find, organise, and use the right study resources. Design a centralised and intuitive learning experience that helps students bring these resources together, organise them by subject or topic, and easily discover what they need for their studies. The design should demonstrate how students can find, organise, navigate, and access different types of learning resources in one seamless experience. 

Explain:

- What is the problem?
  Students must search across multiple platforms to find study notes, PDFs, YouTube videos, GitHub repositories, textbooks, and previous-year question papers.
  This scattered learning experience makes it difficult to discover relevant resources, organize materials by subject, and access everything efficiently.
  
- Who is affected by it?
  College and university students who need resources for exams, assignments, and projects.
  School students who rely on notes, textbooks, and video tutorials.
  Competitive exam aspirants who need organized study materials and previous-year questions.
  Self-learners and developers who use online courses, documentation, and GitHub repositories to acquire new skills.
  
- Why is solving it important?
  Saves time by reducing the need to search multiple platforms.
  Makes discovering relevant study materials easier.
  Helps students prepare more effectively for exams and projects.
  Creates a more accessible and seamless learning experience.
  
- What are the limitations of existing solutions?
  Scattered resources: Different content types are hosted on separate platforms.
  Limited organization: Students often manage links and files manually using bookmarks, folders, or messaging apps.
  Fragmented discovery: Search engines may return overwhelming or irrelevant results instead of a focused collection for a particular subject.
  Platform switching: Students frequently move between websites and apps to study different materials.
  Lack of personalization: Existing platforms may not provide one unified, customizable study space for resources from multiple sources
